01

Why partner

Building something meaningful

A school is built by more than bricks.

DPS Millennium International School is being established in Al Wukair with a long-term focus on learning, opportunity and community.

The right partnerships can extend that mission beyond the classroom — connecting students with people, organisations, expertise and experiences that make education more relevant.

We are interested in relationships that create genuine value for students, the school and the wider community — not simply logo placement.

Partnerships with purpose.

Student first
