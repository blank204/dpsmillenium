The campus

The campus takes shape.

A record of the build in Al Wukair — the design as drawn, and the site as it stands. Architect's impressions and site photographs are labelled separately, so nothing here is mistaken for a finished building.

Construction underway · Tentative opening April 2027

Site photograph · ground-floor columns cast, Al Wukair

Where it stands

Built in the open, on the record.

Every claim on this page is either a drawing or a photograph, and it says which. Progress notes are updated as work is verified on site.

  • Structure risingFoundations are complete and ground-floor columns are cast across the footprint.
  • Tentative opening April 2027The current target. Any formal revision will be published here first.

Site record

Construction, stage by stage.

Photographs from the Al Wukair site, in the order the work happened. Captions describe only what is visible in the frame.

Stage 01

Foundations

Excavation complete, foundation bases and blinding cast across the footprint.

Stage 02

Reinforcement

Slab and column steel tied on site and inspected before each pour.

Stage 03

Columns and formwork

Shutters struck and re-set along each block as the vertical structure goes up.

Stage 04

Ground floor set out

The column grid now reads as the plan — teaching blocks around the central court.

Oversight

Reviewed on site, every stage.

DPS Group representatives, the design team and the contractor meet on site through the build. Programme, quality and safety are signed off stage by stage.

Planned facilities

What the building is for.

Confirmed scope from the current design. Detail is published as each package is signed off, so this list grows rather than changes.

Teaching blocks

Classrooms arranged by phase, from Nursery through Class XII.

Science laboratories

Physics, chemistry and biology labs for the senior school.

Library

A central reading and research space across both wings.

Sport and assembly

Courts, play areas and a covered assembly space.

Facility specifications are indicative until the school publishes final confirmation.
