About the school

A school with no students yet.

DPS Millennium is a CBSE school being built in Al Wukair by the DPS Group of Institutions, Qatar. This page holds what is decided, what is not, and what the crest on the gate is going to mean.

Mission & vision

Service before self — the line the group has taught by for a quarter of a century.

Mission

Teach the CBSE curriculum properly — laboratories that are used, reading that is finished, mathematics that is understood rather than memorised — and expect students to be decent to each other while they do it.

Vision

A school in Al Wukair that families choose on results and on how their children are treated, not on the brochure — and that a student can leave prepared for a university anywhere.

Commitments

Six things worth holding us to.

  1. 01CBSE, taught in fullNo shortcuts around the board syllabus, and laboratory work that actually happens on the timetable.
  2. 02Class sizes we publishWhatever the number turns out to be, it goes on this site rather than into a prospectus footnote.
  3. 03Arabic and Qatar historyTaught to the Ministry's requirement, by teachers hired for it rather than borrowed from another subject.
  4. 04Sport and the arts on the timetableNot an after-school extra for the families who can stay late.
  5. 05Teachers who stayRecruitment and retention are the whole game in a new school, and the first cohort feels it most.
  6. 06Dates that mean somethingIf the April 2027 opening moves, it moves on this page the week we know.

Questions

What families ask first.

When does the school open?+

4 April 2027 is the target, for the 2027–28 academic year. It depends on construction and on Ministry approvals, so treat it as tentative until the school confirms it.

Which curriculum will it follow?+

CBSE, plus the Arabic and Qatar History requirements set by the Ministry of Education. Subject lists and assessment detail follow once staffing is set.

Where exactly is the campus?+

Al Wukair, south of Doha and near Al Wakrah. A precise address, catchment and bus routes will be published closer to opening.

What will fees be, and when can I apply?+

Neither is set. Registering your interest below puts you on the list that gets fee structures and admissions dates first — there is nothing else to apply for yet.

How does this relate to DPS Modern Indian School?+

Same group, separate school. DPS Millennium is the group's third institution in Qatar, with its own campus, staff and admissions — a place at one is not a place at another.
