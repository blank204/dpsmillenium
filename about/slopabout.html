Institutional Purpose

Mission & Vision

DPS Millennium is built on the enduring principle of "Service Before Self" — balancing rigorous academic preparation with moral integrity and deep cultural grounding.

Our Mission

To Educate, Empower & Elevate

To deliver an inspiring CBSE-aligned education that challenges students to think critically, solve real-world problems through scientific inquiry and the arts, and act with unyielding empathy and ethical responsibility in an interconnected world.

Inquiry-Based STEM Character Building Global Readiness
Our Vision

A Benchmark of Excellence

To be recognized as Qatar’s leading center of transformative learning — where every child discovers their unique potential, celebrates diversity as strength, and graduates equipped to lead with courage, wisdom, and humility across the global stage.

Academic Rigour Inclusive Community Lifelong Curiosity

Strategic Commitments

Core Educational Objectives

Six foundational pillars guide our pedagogical roadmap for the April 2027 inaugural cohort.

01

Academic Rigour & Inquiry

CBSE curriculum enriched by experiential STEM laboratories, humanities discourse, and investigative projects that develop deep conceptual clarity over rote memory.

02

Character & Moral Leadership

Instilling timeless ethical frameworks, integrity, and active community service to nurture compassionate leaders who stand up for justice and equity.

03

Holistic Arts & Athletics

Full-scale sports facilities, performing arts auditoriums, visual art studios, and debate societies that cultivate balanced, well-rounded young minds.

04

Bilingual & Cultural Grounding

Deep appreciation for Indian traditions and Qatari cultural heritage alongside comprehensive Arabic and international language programmes.

05

Exemplary Educator Faculty

Recruiting seasoned master educators, providing continuous pedagogical training, and sustaining exceptional student-to-teacher mentorship ratios.

06

Future-Ready Campus Infrastructure

A purposefully designed 25-acre green, energy-efficient campus in Al Wukair featuring smart classrooms, robotics spaces, and immersive libraries.

Institutional Heritage

The DPS Legacy in Qatar

DPS Millennium stands upon more than half a century of academic prestige and community trust across the Arabian Gulf.

  • 1968 Foundation

    Establishment of DPS Modern Indian School, Doha

    The pioneering Indian educational institution in Qatar is founded, setting an unmatched precedent for scholastic excellence, values, and community impact.

  • 2003 Expansion

    Launch of DPS Monarch International School

    Expanding the group’s footprint with modern pedagogical frameworks, international standards, and distinguished board results.

  • 2015 Golden Jubilee

    Celebrating 50 Years of Educational Leadership

    Honoring five decades of alumni who now hold prominent positions in engineering, medicine, diplomacy, and enterprise worldwide.

  • 2026 Groundbreaking

    Construction Begins for DPS Millennium in Al Wukair

    Mobilization and structural works commence for our flagship 21st-century campus, embodying the next generation of institutional learning.

  • 4 April 2027 (Target) Inaugural Cohort

    Doors Open to the First Student Cohort

    Welcoming our foundational students into purpose-built facilities with comprehensive CBSE curriculum and vibrant student life.

  • 2030 Vision Horizon

    Recognized Regional Center for Innovation & Ethics

    DPS Millennium achieves full K-12 maturation, establishing global university partnerships and pioneering regional STEM research programs.

Parent Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers for families considering DPS Millennium for their child's educational journey.

When is the school tentatively scheduled to open? ▾
Our target opening date is 4 April 2027 for the 2027–2028 academic year, subject to the final completion of campus infrastructure and Ministry approvals.
Which educational curriculum will DPS Millennium follow? ▾
DPS Millennium will deliver the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) curriculum, enhanced by innovative STEM, robotics, holistic arts, and Ministry-approved Arabic and Qatar History programmes.
Where is the campus located and how accessible is it? ▾
The school is situated in Al Wukair, State of Qatar, with direct expressway connectivity to Al Wakrah, Doha, Mesaieed, and surrounding residential communities. Dedicated student bus transit networks will be provided.
When will formal admissions and fee structures be published? ▾
Admissions expressions of interest are currently open. Detailed fee schedules, grade group allocations (Kindergarten through Senior School), and assessment criteria will be shared directly with registered families in late 2026.
How does DPS Millennium relate to DPS Modern Indian School? ▾
DPS Millennium is an integral institution within the renowned DPS Group of Institutions in Qatar, sharing our core governance philosophy, educational standards, and proven pedagogical heritage while offering a brand-new 21st-century campus.
